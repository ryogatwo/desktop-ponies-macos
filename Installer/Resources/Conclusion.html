Your ponies are ready

  1. Open System Settings → Screen Saver (System Preferences on older macOS versions).
  2. Select Desktop Ponies 1.7. If it is missing, close and reopen Settings.
  3. Open Options to choose your cast, then click Apply.
  4. Start the preview to enjoy your ponies.

Older Desktop Ponies entries can remain installed. Select version 1.5 to use this update.

To uninstall, remove Desktop Ponies 1.7.saver from your account’s Library/Screen Savers folder.
