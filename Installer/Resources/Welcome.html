Desktop Ponies 1.7

Bring animated ponies to your Mac’s screensaver.

  • 309 characters with a searchable cast picker
  • Mane Six preset: exactly six ponies, one of each character
  • Custom casts and random selections, with up to 40 ponies

Installs for your account in Library/Screen Savers. Your saved cast, sprite size, and background settings are preserved.

Requires macOS 11 or later. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
